Before you ask.

What is Starlight?⌄

An AI day planner. It takes your task backlog and your routine and builds a focused, time-boxed plan for today — so you're not the one deciding what to do next.

How's it different from a to-do app or calendar?⌄

To-do apps hold the list; calendars hold the time. Starlight does the hard part in between — deciding what actually matters today and placing it in the time you really have.

How does it pick what matters?⌄

It weighs urgency and value across your backlog, then fits the top of that list into your free blocks. You can always rearrange before you commit.

Is it free? What will it cost later?⌄

Free throughout the beta. Paid plans come after launch — and beta testers keep 6 months free.
